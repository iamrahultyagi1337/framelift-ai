# FrameLift AI

FrameLift AI is a macOS Apple Silicon app by Rahul Tyagi for AI video frame interpolation. It converts local 30fps videos, including large MKV files, into smoother 60 FPS, 90 FPS, or 120 FPS MP4 exports at 1080p or 4K.

## Features

- Convert 30fps video to 60 FPS, 90 FPS, or 120 FPS.
- Export 1080p or 4K MP4 files.
- Support large MKV workflows with bundled FFmpeg and FFprobe.
- Use RIFE AI frame interpolation for smoother motion.
- Show progress, percentage, and estimated time remaining.
- Run locally on Apple Silicon Macs.
- Include Rahul Tyagi branding and Instagram link.

## Requirements

- macOS 14 or newer.
- Apple Silicon Mac.
- Significant free disk space for temporary AI video processing.

## Important Install Note

This release is an ad-hoc signed macOS app package. For a fully Gatekeeper-notarized public build, the app must be signed with Rahul Tyagi's Apple Developer ID certificate and notarized through Apple.

## Privacy

FrameLift AI is designed for local video processing. Your selected videos are processed on your Mac and are not uploaded by the app.
